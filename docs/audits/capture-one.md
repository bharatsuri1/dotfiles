# Capture One

## Role

Primary professional RAW ingestion, tethering, culling, and editing environment.

## Recommendation

Retain if it remains the authoritative RAW workflow; avoid carrying forward years of opaque preferences wholesale.

## Modern baseline

Choose Catalogs versus Sessions per project deliberately, use referenced/managed originals consistently, maintain verified backups, and export reusable Styles/presets/workspaces through supported mechanisms. Catalog backup does not replace original-image backup.

## Host and legacy audit

Capture One 23 and versioned preferences are installed. No photographic assets or portable presets appear in legacy dotfiles.

## Configuration ownership

Bootstrap/activate separately. Keep licenses, catalogs, sessions, originals, previews, caches, job history, device profiles, and preferences out of dotfiles. A curated export of Styles, recipes, shortcuts, or workspace may live in a separate private assets backup if licensing permits.

## Integration notes

Define ownership relative to ON1, Photomator, and Darkroom. DaVinci may consume exported stills. Vesper should not alter color-managed image surfaces.

## Open decisions

- Choose Catalog/Session policy and identify the small reusable preset set.
